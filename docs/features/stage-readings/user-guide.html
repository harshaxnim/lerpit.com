Readings and controls

Beside every lesson is the stage, where the step you are reading runs. Two small cards sit in its top corners.

Controls, top right

The ↺ button restarts the simulation from the beginning. Any sliders you moved keep their values.

When a step has something you can change, the card is titled Controls. It starts folded, so the drawing has the room. Click the word to show the sliders, and again to fold them away.

Readings, top left

Some steps report live values while they run: total energy, a count, a velocity, the frame rate. A step starts with just its first reading showing, as a small value with no label. Click it to see them all, listed under Readings. Click that word to shrink the card back to one value.

Keep just one. Click any reading in the full card and the card shrinks to that one value. A graph stays a small graph and a bar stays a small bar. Click the value to bring the full card back.

It remembers, for one chapter

What you open, fold or keep lasts through a restart. Each new chapter starts the way it was written to.

Tabs

When a step has more than one view, such as the drawing and a table of numbers, they are tabs at the top right of the stage, on the same line as the lesson's title trail.

On a phone

The stage opens as a drawer from the right edge. The two cards share the top of it, so neither covers the other. Fold one if you want more room for the drawing.
