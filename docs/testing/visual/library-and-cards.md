# Library, collections and cards

Routes: `/library/`, `/meta/`, `/physics-engine/`, `/rendering/`, and the shelves at the foot of every lesson.
Sizes: 1400×900, 390×844.

## Library
- [ ] Heading, one-line description, a rule, then each collection with its cards.
- [ ] Locally, unlisted and draft lerpettes are listed with a corner tag; in public view they are not.

## Collection page
- [ ] Title and body of `collection.md`; links in the body work.
- [ ] Its lerpettes, newest first (or in `order` when control.toml sets one).
- [ ] Below: mixtapes from other collections, then other collections.

## Cassette cards
- [ ] Each collection has its own card style; two collections never share one.
- [ ] Colours read at the brand tone: not darker than the spine, not oversaturated.
- [ ] Paper grain and the press visible but quiet; the title legible over the art.
- [ ] Hover lifts the card; focus ring visible on keyboard focus.
- [ ] Cards wrap to one column at 390 without overflow.
