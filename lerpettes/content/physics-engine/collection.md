# Physics engine

As part of my public learning towards becoming a physics engines expert, I am going to build up from the math and physics, and implement them at various levels of performance, starting from easy-to-read JS implementations to high-performance CUDA kernels.

Inspired by A. W. Bargteil and T. Shinar, [*An Introduction to Physics-based Animation*](https://cal.cs.umbc.edu/Courses/PhysicsBasedAnimation/), ACM SIGGRAPH Courses, 2019.
